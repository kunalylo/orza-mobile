What happens next

  • The status moves from Waiting for Review to In Review. Apple emails you when it changes.
  • If Apple approves, the status becomes Pending Developer Release. Open the version page and click Release This Version when you're ready.
  • Reviewer questions and rejections arrive in App Store Connect › App Review. Reply there; you can also appeal a rejection there.
  • Keep the demo password the same and AI switched on until the review is finished.

If Apple rejects because the account is an individual one

Guideline 5.1.1(ix)

Apple may say apps close to healthcare (ÖRZA has medicine alarms and prescription scans) must come from an organization, not a person. You chose to try the individual account first and accepted this risk.

  1. You can reply or appeal in App Review.
  2. If Apple keeps the rejection, the fix is an Organization account in the Apple Developer Program. It needs a D-U-N-S number for your company.
  3. Then the app moves to that account: App Information › Transfer App. A transfer needs one approved version first, so after a first-time rejection the app is created again in the new account instead.
  4. Talk to Claude before you start. The app's settings must change for a new account.

    Steps (READ_STEPS)counting…

No other data type is read in the background. Health Connect asks the member separately on its own screen; if they refuse, steps are saved only while ÖRZA is open, and the app says so.

First change both stores' privacy answers. Apple: App Privacy › Contact Info › Phone Number, Yes, App Functionality. Google: Data safety › Personal info › Phone number, Yes, optional, App functionality. The WhatsApp card can appear in the app without an update, so the answers must change first.

Only for members who turned "Help improve ÖRZA's AI" on, and never steps or health-app data. Update Apple's App Privacy (add Other Purposes) and Google's Data safety first.
